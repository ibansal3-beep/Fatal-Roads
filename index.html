THE QUESTION

What do national totals hide?

A single number summarizes the scale of the problem, but it does not reveal differences across places, months, hours, or recorded conditions. We begin by looking at geography.

Association is not causation

THE TAKEAWAY

A National Total Is Only the Beginning

Fatal Roads examines the same year of recorded fatal crashes through location, time, conditions, and state-level comparisons. Each view answers a different question; together, they show why a single national total cannot describe every pattern.

These comparisons are descriptive. Without traffic exposure data, we cannot determine which place, hour, or condition is inherently more dangerous. The data also cannot establish what caused any particular crash.
